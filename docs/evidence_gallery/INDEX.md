## render_1218_support_mask.png (1170x1600)

Surfaces traced on PHerc1218 from the m7 prediction, before and after CT-support masking (villa PR #1156, branch `progress/ct-support-mask-1114`), rendered through the standard `vc_render_tifxyz` with ABF++ flattening at level 1, scale 0.5, auto-crop. Three seeds, one row each, original m7 on the left and support-masked m7 on the right, same seed and tracing parameters in both arms. Seeds were picked deterministically on CT-supported prediction 4 to 32 voxels from the phantom halo (rule and coordinates in results/p8_support_mask/r1_seeds.json). The dark regions are where the traced mesh carries no CT under it, so the render has nothing to sample; their extent tracks the measured unsupported-quad fraction printed in each title, 34.1 and 43.5 percent on seeds 0 and 1 against 1.5 percent on seed 2. Masking moved 95 to 98 percent of quads by more than 8 voxels (median 24 to 123), so the two arms are different surfaces, while the unsupported fraction barely moves, which is the visual form of the finding that output masking changes the trace without confining it to CT support. Supports the claims in results/p8_support_mask and the villa 1114 thread.

## support_aware_rule.png (1750x650)

What a support-aware stopping rule would drop, measured on the three P8 traces in patch grid space. Blue is a quad with CT under it in the canonical masked volume, red is a quad with none, black is outside the patch or outside the built support region. The rule applied here keeps supported quads and then runs a 3x3 binary opening to drop isolated survivors; the opening costs only 0.1 to 1.1 percentage points beyond the plain support test, so the supported part of the patch is coherent rather than speckled. Retention is 65, 56 and 97 percent of in-region quads on the three seeds, and removed quads sit at mean grid distance 6.4, 6.7 and 2.5 from the patch edge against 12.6, 13.4 and 11.4 for kept ones. The picture also corrects a loose reading of that statistic. The unsupported quads are not a thin rim; they form one contiguous mass that reaches the patch boundary and extends well inward, which is what growth continuing in a direction the volume does not cover looks like. Data in results/p8_support_mask/r1_trim.json, rule in p9_trim.py.

## ink9um_1218_demo.png (1685x1165)

The released cross-scroll ink checkpoints running end-to-end on a PHerc1218 surface, as a compatibility demonstration and nothing more. The segment is the hazard-weighted trace from the A/B figure above, rendered as a 28-slice surface volume at the scroll's native 8.64 um through vc_render_tifxyz, then passed to the stock flat inference of the new ink_detection package with the scrollprize/ink_9um hybrid checkpoints, both seeds at steps 20k and 75k, forward and reverse. What the panels show is the middle CT slice and the four forward probability maps rescaled per the tutorial's display convention. The response is structured rather than uniform and the four checkpoints partially agree, pairwise correlation 0.42 to 0.62 on valid pixels, which says the model is reacting to something consistent in the render. It does not say that something is ink. No letter shapes are visible, 1218 is outside the four-scroll training corpus, the scroll is 4 to 10 percent finer in resolution than the corpus, and some bright areas track visible surface damage in the CT slice, so surface condition is a live alternative explanation. Depth-window offsets were left at default. Stats and the per-run table in results/ink9um_1218/ink1218_demo.json.

## tightgap1218_bands.png (2210x806)

The tight-contact validation set, one example crop per gap band with the control arm at the right. ## ink9um_w016_arms.png (1800x990)

What the released 9 um cross-scroll ink checkpoints gain from inference-time choices, scored inside the held-out validation mask of pherc0139-w016, one of the three segments the label README says those checkpoints report metrics on. The bar chart puts every arm against the preregistered bar, the shaded band being the median spread between the two training seeds at matched step, 0.033 F1, fixed before any ensemble was scored. Averaging all fourteen checkpoints reaches F1 0.764 against 0.732 for the best single, which lands just under that bar and is therefore reported as indistinguishable rather than as a gain. Repeating the comparison on the other two validation segments gives the same verdict three times out of three, and on PHerc1667 the average is slightly worse than the best single checkpoint, so the ensemble's apparent edge here is a single-segment observation rather than a general one. The two settings that look like tunables only lose: the depth window cannot be widened past the 17 of 21 slices the checkpoint fixes, narrowing it costs heavily, and fusing the forward and reverse passes costs 0.051 F1 because the reverse pass is near chance here at AUC 0.538. The strips below are the label mask and three probability maps over the same region, so the letters are visible and the ensemble's cleaner reconstruction can be compared with the reverse pass that carries almost nothing. Numbers, arms and limits in results/ink9um_w016.

## ridgecentre1218_offsets.png (1750x520)

Whether the repaired PHerc1218 labels sit on the CT ridge, measured on the 209 published contact crops that carry both split instances. The offset is the CT intensity maximum minus the label-run centre, both along a normal oriented from one instance to the other and then flipped away from an assumed winding centre, which makes negative mean the ridge sits inward of the label centre. That assumed centre was a constant I had not checked, so the write-up re-runs the sign split under an axis fitted from the data and under a test that needs no axis at all, and the answer is the same under every one. Three corridors are shown because a magnitude that moves with the corridor is an estimator property rather than a physical one. The medians are 0.00, 0.00 and −0.25 voxels, the bootstrap interval contains zero in all three, and the sign split is 49.8, 49.8 and 51.2 percent negative, so there is no systematic displacement to report. The preregistration named that outcome in advance as its third failure condition. The spread is the part that matters for consumers, half of the per-crop medians sit more than 0.63 voxels from the ridge, which is a floor on how finely anything scored against these labels can resolve placement. Method, the implementation error the frozen failure conditions caught, and limits are in results/ridgecentre1218.
