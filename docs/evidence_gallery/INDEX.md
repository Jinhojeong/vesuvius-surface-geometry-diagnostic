# Evidence gallery (backfill, 2026-08-13)

Before/after evidence images on real scroll data, generated from existing
artifacts on this box. All selections are deterministic (rules stated per
figure). Coordinates are L1 voxels unless noted. Generated by
`make_gallery.py` in this directory; no source artifact was modified.

## repair_1218_before_after.png (1300x1900)

row per site, before on the left and after on the right. Before panels show
the v3 labels from `/mnt/vesuvius/p1218_repair_v3/blocks_repaired` with the
fused instance in red and all other labels gray. After panels show the same

occupying the formerly fused region in blue and orange. Site selection: all

`/mnt/vesuvius/kaggle_p1218_repair_v2/validation/v4_recast_sites.csv.gz`,
sorted by applied voxels (`assigned_A+assigned_B`) descending, at most one
site per tile, tile required in both trees. The three picks are
z224/tile_y1792_x2240 (site z=298 y=2187 x=2498, 8052 voxels, conf 0.114,
id 322 -> 322/219), z10304/tile_y1792_x1792 (z=10507 y=1856 x=2141, 7658

Supports the public claim that the v2.0 repair splits fused sheet instances
at flagged sites on the real PHerc1218 labels, with per-site recast-ray
validation (56.4% of the 109,309 split sites validated).

## labelcov_1218_map.png (1400x1240)

The PHerc1218 label-coverage measurement from
`/mnt/vesuvius/labelcov1218/coverage_tiles.csv` (1321 tiles measured, 1233
with at least 1000 predicted voxels plotted). Three map panels show mean
`coverage_near4` per tile footprint (tile x0+256, y0+256), averaged over
slabs within three z0 bands, [0, 3874), [3874, 7749) and [7749, 11625);
viridis, 0 = dark, white = no tile. The dark outer ring around the bright
center in every band is the outer-wrap label void. The fourth panel is the
per-tile `coverage_near4` histogram, strongly bimodal with peaks at 0 and
1 (514 tiles below 0.1, 661 above 0.9). Supports the claim that the
PHerc1218 sheet labels cover about 45% of m7-predicted surface voxels, that
per-tile coverage is nearly all-or-nothing, and that the uncovered part
concentrates in the outer wraps.

secondary stratum (villa #191).

## m7_beyond_support.png (1400x780)

The published PHerc1218 m7 surface prediction stores large positive
regions where the canonical masked CT volume of the same timestamp
(20250521120456) has no data at all. Panel A is axial plane z=10208 (L1):
the background is the chunk-existence map of the masked volume's level-1
array at chunk-z 79, from a single live S3 key listing (264 of 900 grid
cells stored; light gray = 128^3 chunk object present, dark = no object,
zarr fill 0), and the stored m7-positive voxels of the same plane (level
1, >= 128, read through the coverage run's chunk cache at
`/mnt/vesuvius/hazard_zarr_smoke/m7L1_cache`) are overlaid in red as an
8x box-mean density. The interior spiral sits on gray (supported); the
solid red halo ring sits on dark (no CT data): 36.8% of this plane's
1,627,432 positives are on absent chunks. The yellow box is the
masked_empty void cube z10080/tile_y896_x2688 and the cyan box the
control cube z10080/tile_y1344_x1792, both from
`/mnt/vesuvius/voidct1218/windows_class.csv`. Panel B gives the pool
counts from `/mnt/vesuvius/voidct1218/summary.json`
(pool_chunk_existence): of 730.4M m7-positive voxels across the
1,174-window pool, 46.5% sit in CT chunks absent from the masked volume;
void windows (coverage_near4 < 0.1) are at 85.4%, control windows
(> 0.98) at exactly 0.0%. Supports the claim that the published m7
predictions extend well beyond the masked volume's support, so any
consumer (tracer, mesh extraction, statistics) should intersect them
with CT chunk existence first; same mechanism as the halo reported
upstream in villa #1114 (blend overreach past the mask boundary), for
which PHerc1218 was independently measured at 50.2% voxel phantoms in
that thread.

## render_1218_support_mask.png (1170x1600)

Surfaces traced on PHerc1218 from the m7 prediction, before and after CT-support masking (villa PR #1156, branch `progress/ct-support-mask-1114`), rendered through the standard `vc_render_tifxyz` with ABF++ flattening at level 1, scale 0.5, auto-crop. Three seeds, one row each, original m7 on the left and support-masked m7 on the right, same seed and tracing parameters in both arms. Seeds were picked deterministically on CT-supported prediction 4 to 32 voxels from the phantom halo (rule and coordinates in results/p8_support_mask/r1_seeds.json). The dark regions are where the traced mesh carries no CT under it, so the render has nothing to sample; their extent tracks the measured unsupported-quad fraction printed in each title, 34.1 and 43.5 percent on seeds 0 and 1 against 1.5 percent on seed 2. Masking moved 95 to 98 percent of quads by more than 8 voxels (median 24 to 123), so the two arms are genuinely different surfaces, while the unsupported fraction barely moves, which is the visual form of the finding that output masking changes the trace without confining it to CT support. Supports the claims in results/p8_support_mask and the villa 1114 thread.
